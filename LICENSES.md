## 1. Intellectual Property & Originality Statement

In strict compliance with all copyright and intellectual property guidelines:
- **No Film Assets:** No images, screenshots, audio samples, dialogue recordings, logos, or posters from the film *Sholay* or any other commercial film were extracted or used.
- **Original Cartoon Characters:** The protagonists (Jai & Veeru) are original, stylized comic caricature heroes designed from scratch:
  - *Veeru:* Cartoon adventurer with a red headband, comic mustache, denim vest, and reckless swagger.
  - *Jai:* Cool-headed sidecar companion with aviator sunglasses, stylish hair, leather vest, and sarcastic banter.
  - *Vehicle:* A fictional, vintage terracotta and sunny-yellow frontier sidecar motorcycle with animated spokes, brass headlight, and chrome exhaust pipe.
- **Original Audio & Soundtrack:** All background music and sound effects are 100% procedurally synthesized from first principles using standard Python audio math (`wave`, `struct`, `math`). No copyrighted tunes, melodies, or voice clips were used.
- **Original Dialogue:** All dialogue lines are original, witty buddy-comedy banter written exclusively for this endless-runner experience.
- **Third-Party Trademarks:** The title, characters, and storyline are completely configurable and independent.
